The code

How this was made

The shape. Environment Agency lidar flown in the winters of 2019–20 and 2020–21 gives the shape of the area; a custom deep-learning model sorts its points into ground, buildings, trees and water.

The heat. SOLWEIG (Lindberg et al. 2008) follows the sun over them hour by hour on 13 August 2026 and, with the Met Office's 2 km UKV air temperature, humidity and wind, gives the feels-like temperature (UTCI) on each 1 m square. From a UTCI of 38 °C (very strong heat stress), a walker's core temperature starts to rise within half an hour (Błażejczyk et al. 2013).

Paths, stops and plans. Footpaths are from OpenStreetMap, bus stops from NaPTAN; places built since the flight are greyed. New trees are placed as TreePlanter does (Wallenberg et al. 2022). Urban means the census areas the ONS classes as urban (2021).
